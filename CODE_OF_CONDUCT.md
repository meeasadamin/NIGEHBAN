# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and keep discussion focused on the work. Harassment,
discrimination, and personal attacks are not accepted in issues, pull requests, or any other project space.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact options on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/meeasadamin). Reports will be reviewed promptly and kept
confidential. Maintainers may remove comments or contributions, or block participants, who break this code.
